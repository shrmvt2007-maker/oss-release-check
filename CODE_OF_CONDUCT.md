# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be welcoming and respectful to everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Accept feedback and focus on what helps the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Contact the project maintainers privately through the contact method listed on the repository profile. Reports will be handled discreetly and reviewed fairly.
